I am an **Associate Professor in Scientific Computing** in the
<a href="https://www.it.uu.se/research/scicomp">Division of Scientific Computing</a>,
<a href="https://www.it.uu.se">Department of Information Technology</a>,
at <a href="https://www.uu.se">Uppsala University</a>.

My research is in **numerical analysis and scientific computing for partial differential equations**,
with particular emphasis on developing robust, high-order, and structure-preserving numerical
methods for problems arising in **fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, and kinetic plasma physics**.

Before joining Uppsala University, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor of Mathematics at
<a href="https://www.math.tamu.edu/">Texas A&M University</a>, USA.

---

### Research

My current research is centered around three closely connected areas:

#### Structure-preserving numerical methods

I develop numerical methods that preserve fundamental mathematical and physical properties
of PDE models, including **positivity, invariant domains, conservation laws, entropy inequalities,
and stability**.

Topics include:

- high-order finite element methods,
- summation-by-parts (SBP) finite difference methods,
- invariant-domain and positivity-preserving methods,
- convex limiting and flux-corrected transport,
- residual and entropy viscosity methods,
- stabilized continuous finite element methods.

#### Computational fluid dynamics and MHD

I work on numerical methods for nonlinear hyperbolic and fluid systems, including

- compressible Euler equations,
- compressible and incompressible Navier--Stokes equations,
- ideal and resistive magnetohydrodynamics (MHD),
- high-speed compressible flows,
- shock capturing and nonlinear stabilization,
- implicit large eddy simulation (ILES).

A central goal is to combine **mathematical robustness with high-order accuracy and computational efficiency**.

#### Plasma physics and fusion

A major part of my current research concerns numerical simulation of kinetic plasma models
and fusion processes.

This includes

- Vlasov--Poisson and Vlasov--Maxwell systems,
- kinetic plasma simulation,
- structure-preserving discretizations,
- high-order finite element and SBP methods,
- low-rank approximations,
- large-scale and GPU-accelerated scientific computing.

---

### Additional research interests

I also work on

- multiscale methods and homogenization,
- nonlinear conservation laws,
- parabolic regularization of hyperbolic problems,
- maximum-principle-preserving methods,
- adaptive numerical methods.
